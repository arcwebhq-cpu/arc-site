1. Included deliverables

  • One tailored single-page website based on the approved preview direction. ARC adapts its reusable design system, templates, components, and automation to the client’s brief; the service is not represented as an entirely from-scratch software build.
  • Agreed sections, typically hero, services, differentiation, about, process, client-supplied proof, FAQ, and contact.
  • Responsive layouts for current desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.
  • One primary conversion path: either a lead form routed to one verified email address or one external call, booking, ordering, or reservation destination.
  • Basic accessibility treatment, including semantic structure, keyboard-visible focus, labels, alt text, readable contrast, and touch-sized controls.
  • Page title, meta description, social-sharing metadata, unlisted preview controls, and production indexing configuration.
  • Performance-minded image sizing and front-end implementation.
  • One ARC-controlled claimable Netlify deployment, a private claim invitation, verification of the claimed destination account and exact final deploy, and the production bundle available for deployment to a fresh client-created project when technical separation is requested.

2. Content and proof

ARC may draft and organize website copy from the client’s brief. The client must verify every factual claim before production delivery. Reviews, licenses, certifications, awards, years in business, guarantees, prices, case studies, and measurable results are published only when the client provides exact, authorized source material.

When client photography is unavailable, ARC may use licensed stock imagery as visual direction. Stock imagery is not represented as the client’s completed work, team, location, or customer.

3. Preview revisions and approval

Two revision rounds to the unlisted preview are included before purchase. A round means one consolidated list of feedback submitted together. Included revisions may adjust copy, approved claims, colors, selected images, section order, and layout details within the single-page scope.

Payment confirms approval of the then-current preview for production handoff. After payment, ARC will correct launch configuration issues and any material difference from the approved preview, but a new page, new function, new brand direction, or materially different conversion strategy is additional scope and requires a written quote.

4. Price, tax, and timing

The service subtotal is $5,000 USD. Applicable sales tax is determined from the purchaser’s destination address and added before payment. Checkout must show the service subtotal, tax, and final total before the purchaser accepts and pays. ARC will not make checkout available until required tax registration and automatic-tax configuration are verified.

The target is production delivery within seven business days after ARC has all of the following: full payment, final content and assets, consolidated feedback, verified lead-routing information, and any account or domain access required for launch. The client must complete the claim in the intended business-owned Netlify account. Client delays, wrong-account selection, an exposed invitation, third-party outages, new scope, or incomplete approvals pause the timeline.

5. Ownership, licensing, and project control

After full payment and production handoff, the client owns the custom website copy and project-specific front-end output ARC created for the project, excluding third-party materials and ARC’s pre-existing systems.

ARC retains its pre-existing templates, components, automation, internal tools, methods, and know-how. Third-party fonts, libraries, platforms, and stock assets remain subject to their own licenses and are not exclusive to the client.

Netlify’s creator application may retain limited deploy access to the claimed project. ARC uses that capability only for handoff and in-scope 30-day support, and not afterward without written authorization. The client may instruct ARC to stop further deployments at any time. For technical separation, the client may request the production bundle for deployment to a fresh client-created project or another host.

6. Launch support

ARC includes 30 calendar days of support for reproducible launch-related bugs in the delivered scope. During that period, ARC may use its limited creator-application deploy capability only to correct those in-scope bugs. Support does not include new sections, new integrations, ongoing content changes, hosting administration, SEO campaigns, advertising, or issues caused by third-party changes or client edits.

7. Excluded unless added in writing

  • Netlify account, plan, ongoing hosting, domain registration or transfer, email service, and paid third-party software or usage fees.
  • Original photography, video, logo design, or a full brand identity.
  • Ecommerce, subscriptions, memberships, user accounts, databases, dashboards, or custom applications.
  • More than one page, more than one lead-routing destination, multilingual content, or complex integrations.
  • Ongoing maintenance, content publishing, analytics reporting, SEO campaigns, paid advertising, or conversion optimization retainers.
  • Legal advice, accessibility certification, privacy compliance audits, or client-specific legal documents.

Any addition must be described and priced in writing before work begins.
