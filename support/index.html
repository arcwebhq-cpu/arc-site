Support

Support starts with one email.

ARC handles project and launch-related support by email only. This page does not provide live chat, an online intake form, or emergency support.

Email

arcwebhq@gmail.com

Availability

Email is reviewed periodically. ARC does not offer 24/7 monitoring or an immediate-response guarantee.

What to include

Include the business or project name, the affected page URL, a short description of the issue, and the device and browser where it occurred. Attach a screenshot only if it contains no confidential information.

Email ARC about support

Launch-related bug support

The standard service includes 30 calendar days of support for reproducible launch-related bugs in the delivered five-page scope. New pages, new integrations, ongoing content changes, hosting administration, and issues caused by third-party or client changes are outside that support. See the complete Service Scope.

Protect sensitive information

Do not email passwords, payment-card details, checkout credentials, Netlify claim invitations, API keys, government identifiers, health information, or other secrets. If a claim invitation may have been exposed, say that it was exposed without pasting the credential into the email.

Policies

Information sent for support is handled under the Privacy Policy. Purchase, cancellation, and refund terms are described in the Terms and Refund Policy.
