# ARC paid-pilot validation

ARC has a polished offer and fictional concept previews, but no verified evidence
that qualified businesses will buy the current one-page scope at a $5,000
subtotal. Treat pricing and demand as hypotheses, not facts.

## Before outreach

Complete the adult/legal/tax/sender gates in `manual-activation.md`. Keep the
paused Apollo backlog paused. Do not use scraped testimonials, customer logos,
misleading personalization, fake scarcity, or guaranteed lead/revenue claims.

## Evidence target

Run a deliberately small pilot before scaling automation:

- 10 qualified discovery conversations in one chosen niche.
- 5 consented preview briefs from businesses that match the published scope.
- 3 paid projects at the published subtotal without undisclosed discounts.
- Exact records of source, qualification, preview hours/cost, revision count,
  acceptance/rejection reason, tax, delivery time, support load, refund/dispute,
  and customer permission for any later testimonial or logo.

Do not change price from a tiny or biased sample. Review evidence with the adult
operator. Continue only if delivery quality, margin, refund risk, and buyer value
are credible. Otherwise change the niche, scope, proof, or price transparently
and version the terms before another checkout.

## Minimum honest metrics

Track qualified conversations → consented briefs → previews delivered → previews
approved → paid checkouts → verified handoffs → retained/no-refund at 30 days.
Report counts and denominators. Never present concept previews, opens, page views,
or unverified form events as customers, sales, leads generated, or conversion
results.
