Refund Policy

Free to preview. Clear after purchase.

The preview is free. The $5,000 website is purchased only after approval, with the limited cancellation window below.

Effective August 11, 2026

1. Free preview

There is no fee to submit a preview request or review an ARC preview. If you do not approve it, do not purchase it. No refund request is needed because no payment was made.

2. Paid website cancellation window

A client may request a full refund within seven calendar days after the $5,000 payment only if ARC has not yet delivered the production website, production files, ownership handoff, or completed launch—whichever happens first.

Because ARC may prepare the production handoff promptly after payment, contact ARC immediately if you need to cancel. A request is effective when received at arcwebhq@gmail.com.

3. After production delivery

After ARC delivers the production site or files, transfers ownership, or completes launch, the purchase is nonrefundable except where required by law or where the delivered work materially fails to match the written Service Scope.

If you report a material nonconformity, ARC will first have ten business days after receiving enough detail and access to correct it. If ARC cannot cure the material issue, ARC will provide an appropriate partial or full refund based on the affected portion of the service.

4. Third-party costs

Separately authorized domain, hosting, software, license, or platform costs that have already been paid to a third party are refundable only to the extent ARC can recover them. ARC will identify such costs before incurring them.

5. Refund timing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through Stripe. Bank and card-network processing times vary and are outside ARC’s control.

6. Payment disputes and legal rights

Please contact ARC first so the issue can be investigated quickly. Nothing in this policy removes any right to dispute an unauthorized or incorrect charge, limits non-waivable consumer rights, or requires a client to give up a lawful remedy.

7. How to request a refund

Email arcwebhq@gmail.com with the purchaser name, business name, payment date, Stripe receipt or session reference if available, and the reason for the request. Do not email full card numbers.
